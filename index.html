BILL Rewards Program Analysis

Strategic evaluation of the DivvyPay cashback agreement for Procreate 2 (Fertility & GYN).

Executive Overview

This agreement establishes a cashback revenue stream based on corporate credit card spend. While it presents a low operational risk and no upfront costs, it contains highly specific exclusions that impact medical procurement and gives BILL unilateral power to alter terms.

Document ID: 86B1098F • Status: Evaluated
🟢
LOW RISK
Safe to Sign

The Cashback Engine

Your reward percentage is entirely dependent on your chosen account sweep periodicity (Billing Cycle). To maximize returns on large capital expenditures (like $100k IVF lab equipment), adjust your cycle before the month begins.

The 0% Exclusion Zone

Not all spending generates revenue. Section 1 of the agreement explicitly excludes several transaction types. For a modern fertility practice, these exclusions can significantly diminish expected returns if not monitored.

🌍

Foreign Transactions

Purchasing microscopes, centrifuges, or specialized IVF media from European or Japanese vendors in foreign currencies yields absolutely $0 in rewards and incurs additional cross-border fees.

💳

Third-Party Bill Pay

Routing your commercial facility rent or large domestic distributor invoices through third-party platforms like Melio or Plastiq will disqualify those transactions from earning any cashback.

🔙

Reversed/Declined

Any chargebacks, declined transactions due to credit limits, or refunds for returned expired pharmaceuticals will instantly trigger a clawback of previously awarded cashback.

Structural Traps & Vulnerabilities

While financially low-risk, the legal architecture of this agreement heavily favors the vendor. Two critical clauses dictate the balance of power.

🧟

The "Zombie" Fallback Loop

Section 7 contains a highly unusual termination clause. Canceling this agreement does not sever the relationship; it resurrects a previous contract.

1

Current State

You sign this deal and receive the 1.5% - 1.9% reward tiers.

2

Termination Event

Clinic terminates the agreement via written notice.

3

The Reset

The "Prior Rewards Agreement" automatically activates. You are legally bound to old terms unless you cancel the entire BILL platform.

⚖️

Sole Discretion Risk

"We may condition, restrict, limit or revoke Cashback in our sole discretion."

Impact on Clinical Operations:

Fertility clinics frequently experience "spiky" spending. A sudden mass-purchase of lab consumables or medications could trigger automated fraud or abuse flags within BILL's systems.

Under Section 5, BILL possesses the contractual right to wipe out your rewards balance without providing an objective standard of proof regarding "abuse."

Strategic Recommendation

This document is net-positive for Procreate 2. It creates a revenue stream without imposing multi-year lock-ins or financial liabilities.

  • ✔ Route domestic, high-volume inventory purchases through the card.
  • ✔ Utilize wire transfers instead of the card for international lab equipment.
  • ✔ Switch to a Prepaid Billing Cycle during high-expense months to hit 1.90%.
